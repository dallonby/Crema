CaffeCrema Labs

Espresso, dialed in.

A native iPhone, iPad, and Mac client for the Wendougee LITA espresso machine. Author pressure and flow profiles, watch every shot in real time, dial in new beans with auto-tune, and share your best recipes with the community.

Join the TestFlight beta View on GitHub

Features

Live brew chart

Real-time pressure, flow, and pumped-volume curves over ghost target lines. See the puck fight back as it happens.

Multi-stage profiles

Visual node editor or list form. Each stage independently pressure- or flow-priority. Preinfusion, soak, extraction, tail.

Auto-tune any bean

Three guided shots to dial in a new bean from scratch. Smart recommendations after every pull.

Profile sharing

One-tap upload to the community. crema:// deep links open the recipe in any installed copy of the app.

Shot history

Every brew saved with tasting note and yield. Targeted tweak suggestions for the next pull.

Demo mode

No machine? Replay a real captured shot through every screen, fully interactive, no hardware needed.

Hardware

CaffeCrema Labs talks to the Wendougee LITA-BA, LITA-BR, and DATA-S espresso machines over Bluetooth LE. Other machines aren't supported yet — if there's demand and the protocol's accessible, we may add others.
