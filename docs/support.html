Frequently asked

Which machines does it work with?
Wendougee LITA-BA, LITA-BR, and DATA-S — anything that speaks the LITA Bluetooth LE protocol.

Will it work with [other espresso machine]?
Not yet. The protocol is reverse-engineered from the Wendougee family. We may add others later if there's demand and the protocol's accessible.

I don't have the machine — can I still try the app?
Yes. Tap the Demo chip in the header to switch to replay mode. A real captured shot plays back through every screen.

The auto-tune wizard suggests a different grind size every shot.
That's working as intended — it's converging on a dialed-in recipe across 3 guided shots, narrowing the adjustment each iteration. The saved profile uses your highest-scoring shot, trimmed to your target brew time.

My shared profile didn't sync to my other device.
You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID on both devices and have explicitly imported the profile from the community on each. Cross-device library sync is on the v0.3 roadmap.

The abort button doesn't always stop the brew.
Fixed in v0.2 — was a BLE timing issue (we held coil 150 too long for the firmware to recognise as a momentary toggle). If you're on an older version, update.

How do I delete my account / data?
Email da@byeq.com with your display name. We purge within 14 days. An in-app delete button is coming in v0.3.

The shot pulled way more than my target yield.
The machine's volume meter reads pump volume, not cup yield — a lot of water soaks into the puck before anything lands in the cup. For accurate yield, either use a BLE scale (Bookoo / Acaia support coming soon) or watch your cup and tap Stop manually when it hits target.

Reporting a bug

Include in your email:

  • What you did, what happened, what you expected
  • App version + build number (Settings → About)
  • Machine model (LITA-BA / BR / DATA-S)
  • iOS / iPadOS / macOS version
  • If you can reproduce it consistently, screen recording is gold
